Scope Use Instead of Scheme Surveyor Instructions

This is a report on a pre-refurbishment survey.

The Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012 creates an explicit 'Duty' to manage asbestos in non domestic properties. The scope of this report is to give advice to the duty holder on how they should manage asbestos within this premises.

The purpose of this report is to record the location, the extent, the product type and the type of asbestos located along with the means of identification on the date of the survey for all ACM's within the premises.

The aim of this survey report is to carry out individual risk assessments for each identified occurrence of asbestos containing material.

The main objective of this survey report is to present all of the information collated during the site survey that the duty holder may need to compile an asbestos management plan and in doing so enable the duty holder to comply with Regulation 4 – The Duty to Manage Asbestos in non domestic premises.

This is a refurbishment survey around the areas of the kitchin, bathroom and roof line.

Agreed Exclusions
N/A
Method Use Standard Method Statement

The survey was undertaken in general accordance with HSG264 ‘Asbestos: The Survey Guide’ and the requirements of the Control of Asbestos Regulations 2012.

Samples were analysed by transmitted/polarised light microscopy with centre stop dispersion staining in accordance with HSG248.

All sampling and analysis was carried out by Assisi Environmental under its UKAS accreditation, and the results are reported on the pages that follow.

Deviations
N/A

The current use of the building is a dwelling and prior to this it was the same. The current occupants have been in the building since various and there is no previously known asbestoes within the premises.

The building was constructed in circa 1960s and has 2 floors. The general construction to the main roof is concrete tile on timber, external walls are brick/blockwork plastered internally, the ground floor is made out of concrete, upper floors are timber, internal partitions are constructed out of a mixture of timber stud and block with plaster, the windows and doors are generally mixed timber and upvc and the rain water goods are mixed plastic and metal. The area depicted upon the enclosed drawing is where the survey took place and as such all other areas not shown on the drawing are excluded from this survey.

Additional notes:-
Kitchen, bathroom and roof line surveyed only as per scope.
